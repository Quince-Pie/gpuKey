Reproduction (inside nix develop, from the repository root)
- make PROFILE=release build/gcc-release/tests/capture_fixture
- GPUKEY_RECORDER_DRIVER=DRIVER tools/benchmark-recorder.sh gpukey BINARY FIXTURE NEW_DIR
- Substitute gsr and its binary for the other consumer. Default QP32/depth2;
  GPUKEY_RECORDER_DEPTH=3 reproduces GSR's normal depth.
- GPUKEY_FIXTURE_SIZE=2560x2880 GPUKEY_FIXTURE_RATE=60 GPUKEY_FIXTURE_SECONDS=8
  select the case; GPUKEY_FIXTURE_BUFFER_MAX=2 fixes the source pool at two.
- GPUKEY_RECORDER_MODE=replay GPUKEY_FIXTURE_SECONDS=40 adds two late saves.
- python3 tools/analyze-recorder.py NEW_DIR
- python3 tools/summarize-recorders.py MATRIX_DIR
- confirmation.sh, final-comparisons.sh, latest-confirmation.sh and
  latest-replay.sh preserve exact ordering, binaries, settings and checks.
- GSR Nix build expressions use the pinned workspace nixpkgs and archived clean
  exports. Their /tmp paths are the paths recorded in this experiment.
