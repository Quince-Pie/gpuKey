Vulkan reconstruction standby checkpoint — 24 October 2026 UTC

Delivered behavior
- The direct-RGB Vulkan queue-owned AV1 path deactivates retired slots in the
  existing encode submission, excluding the overwritten slot and inactive slots.
- Compact standby reclaims unused reconstruction views/images only after
  successful readout and an empty issued-output FIFO. Live references remain.
- New input restores capacity lazily. Fatal encoder failures use full close;
  the completed recording prefix remains available with the original error.
- No persistent field, per-packet field or extra retirement submission is added.
  The final patch skips the retirement-list scan on ordinary pictures.
- Existing retain/default static-cadence policies remain available. The new
  reclamation is reached with --gpu-memory compact --static-interval 2.
