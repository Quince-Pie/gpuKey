Authoritative evidence in gpukey-full-range/
- confirmation/: 36 fresh rotating eight-second runs, four variants across
  1080p60, portrait60 and 1080p240, with identical source/CQP/depth policies.
  Each output independently decodes and has consecutive visible identities.
- replay/: twelve 40-second portrait60 runs, three per variant, with two saves
  after the 30-second history window advances. Initial runs remain included.
- confirmation-summary.txt and replay-confirmed-summary.txt preserve CPU means,
  SD, RSS, VRAM/GTT, buffer counts and decoded-identity results.
- run-confirmation.sh and run-replay-confirmation.sh are the executed commands.
  Input manifests pass. Builds, profilers, players and heavy analysis do not
  overlap these timing runs. Boundary frame counts are retained, not normalized
  away. The producer negotiates two buffers for all measured variants.
- native-full-corrected/, native-limited/: actual recorder capture, 16+16 audio,
  replay, static, reconfiguration and missing-PTS checks.
- native-full-asan/: native full-range capture and teardown under ASan/UBSan.
- final-native-full-matrix/: RGB8/RGB10, portrait, forced keys, ordinary/queue
  reconstruction, unsupported pairs and injected submission/export/view failures.
  Vulkan validation uses the documented repaired RGB validation-layer fork.
- av1-check.log and tests/av1_test.c: first-picture matrix/range agreement and
  mismatch rejection, plus geometry/crop/ownership controls, for both profiles.
- final-verification.log: GCC, Clang, ASan/UBSan and all x86-64 flake checks pass.
  aarch64 and other GPU vendors were not built/tested in this continuation.
- scene-full/ and scene-limited/: private 640x480 diagnostic clips and matched
  spatial-frames/JSON files. Sixteen exact source identities give RGB PSNR
  30.094 dB full and 30.000 dB limited. This is not a natural-scene quality claim.
- mpv-pixels.json: 60 exported PNGs per profile have the expected consecutive IDs.
- decoded-patch-series.json: raw patch values remain stable at 0,1,15,30,120.
- mpv-full-verbose.log, mpv-full-swscale.log, mpv-full-exact.log and their PNGs:
  default fast zimg changes full-range export values; accurate mode or swscale

- scene-*/brave-*-playback.json and *-video.png: headless Brave progress, decoded
  WebCodecs tags/YUV samples, renderer information, canvas and compositor output.

Retained failures and qualifications
- native-full/gpu.log records the first fixture abort: its limited-only endpoint
  oracle rejected correct full-range pixels. The oracle was corrected against
  the declared-range contract, with both profiles rerun.
- samply-full/ records the denied kernel-inclusive request at perf_event_paranoid=2.
  The installed vgd35a... wrapper retries with kernel/hypervisor events excluded.
  Its shim is removed from the recorder environment; system policy is unchanged.
- The first full driver k41r0... used the same change with fuzzy patch context;
  the final vpv68... profile applies with zero fuzz and is tested independently.
- Initial spatial.json uses evenly spread decoded indices. spatial-matched.json
  uses exact common source identities for the reported comparison.
- Full range increases measured encoded replay payload/export sizes about 3%.
  Current RGB CPU means are higher than the fixed reference in the longer runs;
  the results do not establish a CPU gain or a general nonregression bound.
- Full range is an explicit precision/storage choice. The default stays VAAPI.
  The conditional 87.890625 MiB image target is unchanged; no universal optimum
  or state-of-the-art claim on every metric is made. The broader goal is active.
