Surface-owned reusable VAAPI reconstructions — 24 October 2026 UTC

Contract and mechanism
- Preserve AV1 settings, input format/timing, reference semantics, source buffer
  count, replay ownership, completed-prefix recovery and explicit memory policy.
- Fixed source: preceding 220-file checkpoint, /tmp/gpukey-before-va-dpb.
- The original driver keeps idle DPB buffers separately from FFmpeg's surface
  pool. Trimming only that pool cannot reclaim the driver-owned images.
- The first surface-owned candidate lost cross-surface reuse and raised active
  VRAM to about 135.21 MiB. It is preserved, not presented as the winner.
- The revision preserves reuse. Active entry: id/order_hint/buffer. Idle entry:
  zero id/owner_surface_id/borrowed buffer. Empty: no buffer. No new record field.
- Surface deletion clears its live/idle DPB entry before destroying the buffer;
  context deletion leaves surface-owned buffers alive until their surface dies.
- FFmpeg's standby policy trims only unused reconstruction pool leases after
  receive returns EAGAIN. gpuKey enters standby after complete GPU readout and
  CPU ownership of the validated independent key.
- Encoder shutdown/reinitialization remains an open alternative for longer idle
  periods; keeping a live reference is a policy, not a universal memory bound.
