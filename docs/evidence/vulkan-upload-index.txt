Synchronous Vulkan transfers, lazy upload pools, and standby staging reclamation — 24 October 2026 UTC

Contract and mechanism
- Preserve AV1 settings, pixel accuracy, reference semantics, native DMA-BUF
  behavior, completed-prefix recovery and explicit memory policy.
- Preceding VAAPI-DPB checkpoint (227 files) is frozen and verified in
  docs/evidence/vaapi-dpb-control.tar.gz, docs/evidence/vaapi-dpb-evidence.tar.gz
  and docs/evidence/vaapi-dpb.sha256, with its source snapshot in
  /tmp/gpukey-before-upload.
- In libavutil/hwcontext_vulkan.c, vulkan_transfer_frame previously waited on
  downloads (!upload) but returned immediately on uploads (upload), keeping a
  software-frame clone (sw_frame_deps) and the host-mapped or pooled FFVkBuffer
  (buf_deps) attached to two FRAME_TRANSFER execution slots until wraparound or
  teardown. That left swf->buf[0] with refcount > 1 on return, pinned two
  simultaneous GTT host-pointer imports (21.10 MiB at 8-bit portrait, 42.19 MiB
  at 10-bit portrait) or two fp->tmp buffers, and retained both across standby.
- nix/ffmpeg-vulkan-transfer.patch waits for exec completion on both transfer
  directions before returning, omits the redundant execution-context sw_frame
  and buf dependency clones, and trims fp->tmp in vulkan_frame_free_cb when an
  AVVkFrame is freed.
- src/media.c sets fc->initial_pool_size = 0, enables queue_dpb = 1 on uploaded
  Vulkan when supported, extends compact_inputs (when !direct) and
  release_idle_inputs to GK_VULKAN with one writable spare while
  m->last && !m->prepared and zero spares in standby, frees m->staging in
  compact standby when !m->prepared and restores it via ensure_staging, and
  collects completed output before the pre-send pending-packet check on
  uploaded paths (!m->source.dmabuf). No struct field is added: media/options
  remain 2,280/192 bytes on x86-64.
- Naive zero-spare active trimming on uploaded Vulkan is preserved and rejected:
  it causes 129 input-frame allocations and 131 trims across 239 frames due to
  per-frame VkImage churn, whereas the selected one-spare active / zero-spare
  standby policy performs 4 input-frame allocations (2 initial + 2 restored
  after standby), 5 reconstruction allocations and 5 standby trims.
